Medical Implants & Equipment

Our Expertise

Partnering for Healthcare Innovation

Exigencies specializes in partnering with global brands and manufacturers of medical implants and equipment, representing both new and refurbished products in the healthcare market.

We work closely with manufacturers to strategize market entry, brand positioning, and product sales, ensuring effective reach to hospitals, clinics, and healthcare institutions.

Our services include end-to-end support, covering marketing, distribution, sales facilitation, technical coordination, and after-sales service to ensure seamless delivery and performance of medical technologies for end users.

01

🤝  Brand & Manufacturer Partnerships

Exigencies collaborates with global OEMs and medical technology manufacturers to represent and expand their presence in the Indian healthcare market.

Our partnership models include:

  • Exclusive representation
  • Non-exclusive distribution
  • Stocking and supply partnerships
  • Agency representation
  • Co-promotion models

We support partners through brand protection strategies, regulatory alignment, and active participation in medical trade shows and industry events, ensuring strong brand positioning and market visibility.

🔧  End-to-End After-Sales Service

Exigencies provides a complete lifecycle service model for medical devices and equipment.

Service Coverage:

07

📋  Regulatory & Compliance

We ensure all products comply with Indian medical device regulations and post-market surveillance requirements.

Regulatory Coverage:

  • Medical Device Rules (MDR) 2017 – Class A to Class D device pathways
  • CDSCO SUGAM portal registrations
  • Post-Market Surveillance (PMS) & Periodic Safety Update Reports (PSUR)
  • NPPA price control compliance
  • Regulatory vigilance and reporting

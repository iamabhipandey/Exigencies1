Governing Our Service Commitments

How Exigencies Mercantile operates, delivers, and maintains obligations across all engagement categories — for transparency with every partner.

Market Representation

Exigencies Mercantile India Private Limited acts as authorised representative for brands, OEMs, and international principals in India. The scope, territory, exclusivity, and commercial terms of any representation engagement are governed by a signed Representation Agreement. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, all representation is non-exclusive unless specifically designated.

  • The principal retains full ownership of its brand, IP, and product specifications
  • Exigencies Mercantile acts within the scope defined by the signed agreement
  • All market communication requires principal approval prior to release
  • Representation fees, commissions, and expense reimbursements per the commercial schedule
  • Territorial boundaries and exclusivity rights are explicitly defined per engagement
  • Performance reporting is delivered at agreed intervals throughout the engagement
  • Either party may terminate with the notice period specified in the agreement
  • Sub-representation or sub-licensing requires explicit written consent from the principal

Trade Facilitation

All trade facilitation activities are conducted in compliance with applicable Indian import/export regulations, FSSAI requirements, GST provisions, and international trade norms. The company acts as agent, principal, or distributor as defined for each specific transaction.

  • All goods are subject to quality verification and documentation protocols
  • Import and export transactions comply with DGFT, Customs, and FSSAI regulations
  • Payment terms, delivery conditions, and liability limits specified per commercial agreement
  • Exigencies does not guarantee price or availability beyond confirmed purchase orders
  • Returns, claims, and disputes resolved per the dispute resolution clause in each agreement
  • Force majeure events are governed by provisions in the applicable commercial agreement
  • All cold chain and perishable goods subject to additional handling protocols
  • Export consignments subject to applicable DGFT export policy and customs declarations

Market Intelligence

Market intelligence, research reports, industry analyses, and competitive assessments are prepared in good faith based on available information. These materials are intended for the internal strategic use of the commissioning client and are strictly confidential.

  • All market intelligence reports are confidential — not to be shared without written consent
  • Reports are based on publicly available data, industry sources, and network insights
  • Exigencies does not warrant completeness or absolute accuracy of market intelligence
  • Clients may use intelligence for internal planning purposes only
  • Intellectual property in reports remains with Exigencies unless otherwise agreed
  • Reports reflect conditions at time of delivery; subsequent changes are not covered
  • Intelligence does not constitute guaranteed forecasts or legally binding assessments
  • Ongoing intelligence monitoring is a separately contracted service

Regulatory Advisory

Regulatory advisory is provided as guidance based on current laws and regulations across FSSAI, BIS, CDSCO, GST, DGFT, Companies Act, and sector-specific frameworks. Clients are responsible for obtaining independent legal counsel before making compliance decisions.

  • Advisory is based on laws and notifications current at time of delivery
  • Clients must verify requirements with authorised legal and statutory advisors
  • Exigencies Mercantile is not a law firm and does provide legal opinion
  • Covers: FSSAI, BIS, CDSCO, GST, DGFT, Companies Act frameworks
  • Regulatory changes post-delivery are not covered unless contracted for monitoring
  • Advisory on medical devices follows CDSCO Medical Device Rules 2017
  • Cosmetics advisory follows BIS and Drugs & Cosmetics Act provisions
  • Sector-specific advisory scope defined at engagement commencement

Distribution Strategy

Distribution strategies, channel plans, and go-to-market frameworks are tailored to each client's product, market, and commercial objectives. All distribution partnerships facilitated by Exigencies Mercantile are entered into on behalf of the principal only upon explicit written authorisation.

  • Distribution channel design based on product category, geography, and commercial feasibility
  • Distributor and dealer identification subject to client's final approval and agreement
  • Exigencies provides ongoing monitoring and management of channel performance
  • All channel agreements comply with Indian competition and trade laws
  • Distribution strategy deliverables are confidential and for client's exclusive use
  • Exclusive territory rights for distributors require explicit principal authorisation
  • Performance guarantees by individual distributors are not warranted by Exigencies
  • Channel restructuring or rationalisation requires 60-day notice per agreement terms
